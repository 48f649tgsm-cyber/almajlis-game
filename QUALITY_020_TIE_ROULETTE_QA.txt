ALMAJLIS QUALITY 020 — TIE-BREAK CATEGORY ROULETTE
JS syntax: PASS

Tie-break flow:
1. Match ends level.
2. "سؤال الحسم" screen opens.
3. All six match categories are shown visually with their category art.
4. A spotlight cycles across the categories while original WebAudio action music plays.
5. The spotlight gradually slows down.
6. It stops on a random category that has an eligible unused valid 500-point question.
7. The selected category glows and is announced.
8. After a short dramatic pause, the 500-level tie-break question appears.
9. 30-second tie-break timer; answer remains manual.
10. If neither team answers, "تدوير الفئات من جديد" runs the category roulette again and selects a new eligible tie-break question.

No copyrighted music is embedded; the action cue is synthesized live in the browser.
Existing Studio 019 features retained.
Question bank and category images unchanged.
